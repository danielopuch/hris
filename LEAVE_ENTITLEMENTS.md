# Staff Leave Entitlements - HRIS System

## 📋 Available Leave Types

Based on the current HRIS system configuration, staff members are entitled to the following types of leave:

---

### 1. 🏖️ **Vacation Leave (Annual Leave)**

**Entitlement:** 20 days per year

**Description:**
- Paid time off for rest, relaxation, and personal activities
- Also known as Annual Leave or Holidays
- Typically used for vacations, family time, or personal pursuits
- Should be planned in advance

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Request in advance (recommended 2 weeks notice for 1-5 days, 1 month for longer periods)
- Subject to approval by supervisor
- Unused days may carry over (subject to company policy)
- Cannot exceed available balance

**Example Use Cases:**
- Family vacation
- Extended holiday travel
- Wedding attendance
- Personal time off

---

### 2. 🤒 **Sick Leave**

**Entitlement:** 10 days per year

**Description:**
- Paid time off for illness or medical appointments
- Covers personal health issues that prevent work attendance
- May require medical certificate for extended periods (typically 3+ consecutive days)

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Notify supervisor as soon as possible (preferably before work start time)
- Medical documentation may be required
- Not for caring for sick family members (use Personal Leave)
- Cannot be planned in advance except for scheduled medical procedures

**Example Use Cases:**
- Flu or cold
- Medical/dental appointments
- Recovery from surgery
- Short-term illness

---

### 3. �‍👶 **Paternity Leave**

**Entitlement:** 5 days per leave period

**Description:**
- Paid time off for male staff following the birth or adoption of a child
- Allows fathers to bond with newborn/newly adopted child
- Supports family during critical early period
- Typically taken within first few weeks after birth/adoption

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Provide advance notice when possible (birth date)
- Medical certificate or birth notification may be required
- Can be taken consecutively or split (subject to approval)
- Must be used within specified period after birth/adoption

**Example Use Cases:**
- Birth of a child
- Adoption of a child
- Supporting partner during recovery
- Bonding with newborn
- Family adjustment period

---

### 4. 👩‍🍼 **Maternity Leave**

**Entitlement:** 60 working days (approximately 12 weeks)

**Description:**
- Paid time off for female staff for pregnancy, childbirth, and postnatal recovery
- Uganda Labour Law standard entitlement
- Protects employment rights during maternity
- Allows mother to recover and care for newborn

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Provide medical certificate confirming pregnancy and expected delivery date
- Typically taken: 4 weeks before due date + 8 weeks after delivery
- Can be adjusted based on medical advice
- Medical certificate required to extend if complications arise
- Job protected during maternity leave period

**Example Use Cases:**
- Pregnancy and childbirth preparation
- Postnatal recovery
- Newborn care and bonding
- Breastfeeding establishment
- Medical complications related to pregnancy

**Documentation Required:**
- Medical certificate from registered practitioner
- Expected delivery date confirmation
- Birth certificate (after delivery)
- Extension requests require medical justification

---

### 5. 💼 **Unpaid Leave**

**Entitlement:** Available as needed (subject to approval)

**Description:**
- Leave without pay for extended absences
- Used when all paid leave is exhausted
- Must be approved by management
- No salary is paid during unpaid leave period

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Requires advance notice and approval
- Used only when paid leave is insufficient
- May affect benefits during extended periods
- Should be discussed with HR and supervisor

**Example Use Cases:**
- Extended personal travel after paid leave exhausted
- Long-term personal projects
- Sabbatical or career break
- Extended family care situations
- Educational pursuits not covered by company programs

## 🔄 Leave Request Process

### Step 1: Check Balance
- Login to HRIS system
- Navigate to Profile or Leave section
- View current leave balance

### Step 2: Submit Request
- Select leave type
- Enter start and end dates
- Calculate days requested (system auto-calculates)
- Provide reason/description
- Submit for approval

### Step 3: Approval Workflow
- Request goes to immediate supervisor
- Supervisor reviews and approves/rejects
- Employee receives notification
- Approved leave is reflected in calendar

### Step 4: Status Tracking
- **Pending** - Awaiting supervisor review
- **Approved** - Leave confirmed, days deducted from balance
- **Rejected** - Request denied, days returned to available balance

## 📅 Important Notes

### Public Holidays
- Uganda public holidays are separate from leave entitlements
- 12 public holidays in 2025 (already configured in system)
- Staff do not need to request leave for public holidays
- If a public holiday falls on a weekend, observe company policy for compensation

### Leave Year
- Leave year runs January 1 - December 31
- Balances reset annually (check company policy on carry-over)
- Unused leave may be forfeited (subject to policy)

### Overlapping Leave
- Cannot request leave on dates already covered by an approved request
- Cannot exceed available balance
- System validates against public holidays

### Weekend Counting
- System typically counts only working days (Monday-Friday)
- Weekends within leave period may not count toward days (verify with HR)

## 💡 Best Practices

### For Vacation Leave:
1. ✅ Plan major holidays early in the year
2. ✅ Submit requests well in advance
3. ✅ Consider team workload when selecting dates
4. ✅ Coordinate with colleagues to ensure coverage

### For Sick Leave:
1. ✅ Inform supervisor immediately when unable to work
2. ✅ Obtain medical certificate if required (3+ days)
3. ✅ Keep HR updated on extended illness
4. ✅ Don't abuse sick leave - it's for genuine illness

### For Paternity Leave:
1. ✅ Notify supervisor of expected birth/adoption date
2. ✅ Provide birth certificate or adoption papers
3. ✅ Take within reasonable time after birth/adoption
4. ✅ Can be split or taken consecutively (subject to approval)

### For Maternity Leave:
1. ✅ Provide medical certificate with expected delivery date
2. ✅ Notify employer at least 1 month before intended start date
3. ✅ Standard: 4 weeks before + 8 weeks after delivery
4. ✅ Submit birth certificate after delivery
5. ✅ Medical extensions require doctor's certificate

### For Unpaid Leave:
1. ✅ Discuss with HR before requesting
2. ✅ Understand impact on salary and benefits
3. ✅ Provide substantial advance notice
4. ✅ Have clear return date
